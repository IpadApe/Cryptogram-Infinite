Cryptogram Infinite — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-10

Cryptogram Infinite is an offline word-puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information itself. All game progress is stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To serve ads, Google may collect and process your device's advertising identifier and related technical data. In regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before any ads load, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can withdraw or change consent at any time from the ad settings dialog.

Google's handling of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is handled by Google Play Billing. The app receives only whether the purchase is owned; it does not see your payment details.

Network

The app downloads a small daily-puzzle file from a public GitHub repository. These requests include only your IP address, as with any web request, and no identifiers set by the app.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. There is no server-side data to delete.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/IpadApe/Cryptogram-Infinite.
